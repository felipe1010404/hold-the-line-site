Common questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Open Settings → Account and sign in with Google or Apple. Sign in with the same account on the new phone and your progress comes back. Or make a transfer code in Settings on the old phone and enter it on the new one (codes last one day).

I bought the Supporter pass. How do I get it back after reinstalling?

Open the Supporter screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought it with.

How do I cancel the Supporter pass?

Subscriptions are managed by the store. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. You keep the perks until the end of the month you paid for. For refunds, ask Google Play or Apple; we can't issue store refunds ourselves.

Does the Supporter pass make me stronger?

No. It removes ads and adds cosmetic styles and a badge. Every tower, hero and upgrade is earned by playing.

The game is slow or looks wrong on my phone.

In Settings, tap Graphics until it says 2D. The 2D board is lighter on older phones. If it still happens, send a bug report from Settings so we can see your device.

Someone is being abusive in chat.

Tap their message and choose Report, or block them. Messages that several players report are hidden automatically and reviewed.
