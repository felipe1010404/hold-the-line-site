Every match plays out differently

Each level up deals you three cards. Pick one, place it, and build a defense that's yours.

101 towers

Archers, cannons, lightning, frost and stranger things. Merge matching towers to rank them up.

12 heroes

Pick a hero who walks onto the road and fights beside your towers, with a special power of their own.

Cards and skills

Meteors, blizzards, walls, extra road, moving a tower. Every pick changes the match.

26 bosses are waiting

The ogre, the hydra that grows its heads back, the storm roc, the Pumpkin King and more. 106 kinds of enemies across 130 chapters.

Campaign and Endless

Push through the chapters on four difficulty tiers, then see how many waves you can last in Endless.

Events every season

13 events through the year, from Harvest Moon to Frostfall and Pirate Week, each with its own foes, boss and rewards.

Clans and leaderboards

Join a clan, chat, take on clan sieges together and climb the weekly boards.

Fair play, always

Hold the Line is free. Every tower, hero and upgrade is earned by playing. Nothing that makes you stronger is ever for sale.
